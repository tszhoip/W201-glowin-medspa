## FAQ_1_TITLE
Cancellation Policy

## FAQ_1_BODY
We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice are subject to a $75 fee. Monday appointments must be canceled by Saturday at 2 PM. By booking, you agree to this policy. Thank you for understanding.

## FAQ_2_TITLE
Appointment & Arrival

## FAQ_2_BODY
Please arrive 10–15 minutes early to check in and complete any forms. If it's your first visit, plan for a short consultation before treatment. Arriving late may shorten your session so that we can stay on schedule for the next guest.

## FAQ_3_TITLE

## FAQ_3_BODY
